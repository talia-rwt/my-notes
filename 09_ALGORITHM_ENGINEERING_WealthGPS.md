### 2. BSR Mechanics (the physics everything else obeys)
- BSR = time-weighted rolling decay. Yesterday's score halves, today's adds on top. Updates every 1-3h.
- Four recalc windows (EST): **2-4 AM (major)**, 8-10 AM, 2-4 PM, 8-10 PM.
- Sales clusters spike rank, decay bleeds it. Twice as many sales to HIT a rank as to MAINTAIN it.
- Strategy: **Spike + Floor.** One concentrated daily push rotated across recalc windows, plus a 24/7 baseline so the 2 AM major recalc never catches zero velocity.

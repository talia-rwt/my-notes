## **1️⃣ PARALLEL VS SEQUENTIAL — THE ANSWER IS NEITHER. IT'S WEIGHTED PARALLEL.**

Here's the problem with both approaches:

**Equal parallel rotation** (every other visitor sees a different keyword):

* If you're doing 100 SFB sales/day across 10 keywords \= only 10 signals per keyword per day  
* Amazon needs a **critical mass** of search-to-purchase events to move the needle on any single keyword  
* You'd be spreading too thin — no keyword hits escape velocity

**Sequential** (one keyword per week):

* You rank keyword \#1 fast, but while you're focused on it, keywords \#2-7 get ZERO training  
* And here's the real risk: keyword ranking has its own time-decay. By the time you circle back to keyword \#1 in week 8, you've lost ground if sales stopped coming through that search term

### **The Optimal Play: Dynamic Weighted Parallel Rotation**

Run ALL keywords simultaneously but with **unequal traffic distribution** — your highest-value keywords get the lion's share:

| Tier | Keywords | % of SFB Traffic | Signals/Day (at 100 SFB sales/day) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **🥇 PRIMARY** (1 keyword) | Your \#1 scoring keyword | **40%** | **40 signals/day** |
| **🥈 SECONDARY** (2 keywords) | Your \#2 and \#3 | **20% each** | **20 signals/day each** |
| **🥉 TERTIARY** (4-7 keywords) | Remaining keywords | **5% each** | **5 signals/day each** |

**Then every 2-3 weeks, PROMOTE based on ranking data:**

* Check which keywords you're now ranking Page 1 for  
* Any keyword that hits Page 1 top 5 → **demote it to tertiary** (it's self-sustaining now through organic search)  
* Promote the next highest-value keyword that HASN'T cracked Page 1 yet → **elevate to primary**  
* This creates a **rolling conquest system** — you're always concentrating maximum firepower on the keyword that needs it most while maintaining the ones you've already won
